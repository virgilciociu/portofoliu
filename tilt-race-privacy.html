Privacy Policy – Tilt Race by CSLab

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Summary

Tilt Race is a racing game. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not collect, sell or share personal information. The only thing it does online is look up which country you are in, so it can show the game in Spanish or English.

Internet use: language by country

When the game starts, it sends one request to the free service api.country.is. Like any internet request, this request reveals your device's IP address to that service. The service answers with a two-letter country code (for example GB or ES). The game uses that code only to choose between Spanish and English, and saves the chosen language on your device. The game itself does not store or send your IP address anywhere else. If there is no internet connection, the game uses your phone's language instead.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked cars and tracks, coins, garage parts and colours, best times), your settings (music, sound effects, difficulty, controls) and the chosen language are saved only on your device. They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Permissions

  • Internet – only for the country lookup described above.
  • Vibrate – short vibrations when your car hits a wall, uses nitro or drops a bomb.

Advertising and in-app purchases

The game shows no advertising and has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by racing and have no real-world value.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version handles data differently (for example to show ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
